Before you start: recording and automatic answers are off by default. Use only where assistance is permitted and obtain required consent. The installer is unsigned; verify its checksum and follow your organization's security policy.

Three speech presets. Included offline.

Fast

Multilingual Tiny · Q5_1 · 30.7 MiB

Lower local compute and memory. Start here on a slower computer, then check the transcript.

Balanced

Multilingual Base · Q5_1 · 56.9 MiB

The default starting point. A middle ground between resources and recognition quality.

Higher accuracy

Multilingual Small · Q5_1 · 181.3 MiB

More capacity and higher CPU/latency cost. Accuracy is never guaranteed for every utterance.

No separate model download or Python installation is needed for these presets. Online answers still require your own supported provider account; extra model slots use extra requests.

Designed for explicit choices

Audio and languages

Select microphone, playback or supported application audio. Choose speech and answer languages independently. Review and correct transcripts.

Overlay and controls

Separate Pin and Top controls, themes, custom colors, transparency and global shortcuts. Tray and recovery keep it usable.

Models and timing

Choose exact available models or Auto. Enable speech-pause answers deliberately, with manual fallback and usage limits.

Understand the limits

The interface is currently English. Automatic turn/question detection is heuristic. Real provider/OAuth flows and recipient meeting capture paths are not comprehensively verified. Documented Windows capture exclusion is path-dependent; no universal invisibility or undetectability is promised.
